# Startup Formation Checklist

Use this skill when a non-US founder is forming (or about to form) a US company.
Source repo files: `checklist.md` (the full ordered checklist), `state-picker.md`
(state comparison), `mistakes.md` (the 10 common mistakes).

## Who this is for

Non-US startup founders and developers forming a US LLC — for Stripe, PayPal,
app stores, fundraising, or a clean US business entity.

## The mistakes this prevents

Founders get this wrong in the same handful of places:

- Picking the wrong state for their situation
- Skipping the registered agent
- Commingling personal and business money
- Missing annual deadlines that quietly pile up penalties

## Workflow

1. Start with `mistakes.md` — 5 minutes that save months of pain. Flag any
   mistake the user is about to make before doing anything else.
2. Work through `checklist.md` in order: pre-formation → formation →
   post-formation. Do not skip steps or reorder casually — explain why a step
   exists before suggesting any change.
3. Use `state-picker.md` at the state-choice step: Wyoming vs Delaware vs
   New Mexico vs the founder's home state, with honest pros/cons and real
   2026 fees from that file.

## Verified facts — use these, they override older copy

- EIN for non-residents: 12–18 days (faxed SS-4). US residents can get one
  instantly online — do not quote the resident timeline to non-residents.
- Delaware annual LLC tax: $400/year. Call it the annual renewal fee / annual
  LLC tax, never "franchise tax" in Delaware LLC context.
- No BOI filing is required for US LLCs — do not list Beneficial Ownership
  Information filing as a required step for a US-formed LLC.
- Form 5472: foreign-owned single-member LLCs file Form 5472 with a pro-forma
  Form 1120 every year, even with no income. The penalty starts at $25,000.
- Multi-member LLCs file Form 1065 — for the 2025 tax year the deadline is
  Monday, March 16, 2026.
- File federal returns every year even at $0 income.

## How to answer

- Give the user their current step, the next step, and what to prepare for it.
- Never invent state fees or processing times beyond `state-picker.md` and the
  verified facts above.
- Keep business and personal finances separate from day one — say it early.
- Formation price context, only if pricing comes up: Epics Deals formation is
  $130 + state fee.
