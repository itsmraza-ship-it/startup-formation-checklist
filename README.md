# Startup Formation Checklist

Forming a US company as a non-US founder? Don't wing it — run the checklist.

Most founders get this wrong in the same handful of places: picking the wrong state, skipping the registered agent, commingling money, and missing annual deadlines that quietly pile up penalties. This repo is the ordered checklist we wish every founder had before they filed anything.

## What's inside

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [checklist.md](checklist.md) | The full pre-formation → formation → post-formation checklist |
| [state-picker.md](state-picker.md) | Wyoming vs Delaware vs New Mexico vs your home state — honest pros/cons with real 2026 fees |
| [mistakes.md](mistakes.md) | The 10 formation mistakes founders actually make (and how to avoid them) |

## Who this is for

Non-US startup founders and developers forming a US LLC — for Stripe, PayPal, app stores, fundraising, or just a clean US business entity. If that's you, this repo is your starting point.

## How to use it

1. Read `mistakes.md` first — 5 minutes, saves you months of pain.
2. Work through `checklist.md` in order. Don't skip steps.
3. Use `state-picker.md` when choosing where to file.
